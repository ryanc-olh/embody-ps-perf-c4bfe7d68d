How these numbers are calculated
Reading the By Day tables
  • The grayed rows at the top are the prior week, shown for day-over-day comparison. Each week closes with its own total row; weekly figures and tiles always cover the most recent 7 complete days.
Voice
  • Inbound Volume — calls that reached a queue, including callbacks requested through the phone menu. Calls that resolve in the menu without reaching a queue are not counted.
  • Answered — calls where an agent connected.
  • % Answered ≤30s — the service-level measure: of live calls offered, the share that left the queue within 30 seconds — answered by an agent or disconnected by the caller. Hang-ups inside 30 seconds count as met; abandons after 30 seconds count against it. Avg Speed of Answer — the average wait before pickup, across calls that were answered. Callbacks are excluded from both: a callback’s wait is the time until we ring back rather than hold time.
  • Abandonment — counted in patients, not calls: of the patients who reached a queue, the share who never connected with an agent and never received a callback attempt. A patient only counts at all if they connected, took a callback, or waited more than 30 seconds — someone whose only calls were ≤30-second hang-ups is excluded entirely. Each patient counts once however many times they call.
Email
  • Received / Resolved / Net — tickets in, tickets closed, and Received minus Resolved. Patient messages only — automated notifications, marketing, vendor, and system email are excluded (classified from message content).
  • First Response ≤4hr / ≤24hr — share of tickets whose first reply went out within that window, measured from when the email arrived.
  • Open backlog — tickets still open at the end of the window.
Chat
  • Inbound Volume — chat sessions that reached a queue, counting only chats arriving 6am–7pm CT. % Answered ≤30s — same service-level measure as voice (left the queue ≤30s, answered or disconnected); chats not answered the same day are excluded from SL and speed-of-answer but still count as volume.
Satisfaction
  • Satisfaction scores — post-call surveys on a 1–5 scale; “% 4–5” is the share rating 4 or 5 (satisfied).
Partial day (*)
  • Rows and tiles marked * cover today so far and are excluded from weekly totals. Feeds refresh at different rates, so each shows its own “as of” time.
All figures cover the stated window, Central Time.
